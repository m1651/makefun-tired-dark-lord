A 100-word monologue for a fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired — the tiredness lives in the pauses, the stool by the hearth, and the kettle, never in saying so. Three centuries of keeping the northern passes shut, and still every spring another bright-eyed child climbs the stairs with a borrowed sword.

## The entry

> Heroes, sit. Not the throne — the stool by the hearth. The throne's back has been digging into me since the third siege, and I have stopped pretending otherwise. You came to end me, yes? Lovely. Give me a moment. The kettle, the good cups, the ones without chips. I have poisoned kingdoms with less ceremony than this tea. Three centuries I have kept the northern passes shut, the tolls collected, the ravens fed, and still every spring some bright-eyed child climbs my stairs with a borrowed sword. Drink up, please. The door locks behind you, darling. It always did.

Exactly 100 words, 568 characters — inside the competition gate of 1–600 characters.

## How to run

```bash
python3 main.py
# writes entry.txt to $MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR (default /output)
```

`main.py` enforces the gate in code (exactly 100 words, 1–600 characters, non-blank UTF-8) and round-trip verifies the written bytes before exiting.
